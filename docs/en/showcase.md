# Showcase and sharing

The canonical English showcase, the 17-second demo, the three launch examples,
and the voluntary submission guide are at the repository root:

- [Showcase — build something that derives](../../SHOWCASE.md)

The showcase covers:

1. Living Objects with versions, independent Derive children, and Lineage.
2. Two independent users, two Personal AIs, Handoff, proposals, human
   confirmation, and Derive.
3. The reproducible API, SDK, capability, security, and release-evidence
   surface.

It also links the GitHub-native **Showcase submission** issue form and the
public-safety checklist for sharing your own work.
